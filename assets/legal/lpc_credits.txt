Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator
Source: https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator
Generator: https://liberatedpixelcup.github.io/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator/

This game includes LPC-derived character sprite artwork. The original LPC
project artwork is distributed under one or more open licenses, including
CC-BY-SA 3.0, GPL 3.0, OGA-BY 3.0, CC-BY, and CC0 depending on the selected
asset layers.

The LPC artwork was contributed as part of the Liberated Pixel Cup project from
OpenGameArt.org. Detailed upstream authors, source URLs, and per-file licenses
are documented by the Universal LPC project's CREDITS.csv:
https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator/blob/master/CREDITS.csv

Local generated LPC sprite folders also include per-unit metadata.json and
credits.txt files beside the exported PNGs.

Important license handling:
- Credit is required for non-CC0 LPC artwork.
- Modified or combined CC-BY-SA artwork must preserve share-alike terms.
- If a platform uses DRM or asset encryption, review the selected LPC asset
  licenses before release.
