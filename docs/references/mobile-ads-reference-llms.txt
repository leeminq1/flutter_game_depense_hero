Primary sources:
- https://developers.google.com/ad-manager/mobile-ads-sdk/flutter/quick-start
- https://developers.google.com/ad-manager/mobile-ads-sdk/flutter/rewarded
- https://developers.google.com/ad-manager/mobile-ads-sdk/flutter/privacy

Distilled notes:
- Google recommends initializing the Mobile Ads SDK early in app startup.
- Rewarded ads should grant rewards only through the reward callback and should be tested with dedicated test ad units.
- Privacy flow should call `requestConsentInfoUpdate()` on launch and use `canRequestAds()` before requesting ads.
- Interstitial-style formats are intended for natural transition points.

Applied to this repo:
- Ads are planned only at safe boundaries such as post-stage results or optional revive/reward surfaces.
- Reward persistence is modeled transactionally in the local schema and reliability docs.
