## AI Audio Prompt Suggestions

### Stage BGM: early campaign

Create a seamless loopable fantasy tower defense background track, 70-85 BPM, light percussion, lute and pizzicato strings, calm but tactical, readable under constant combat SFX, no vocals, no hard transients, 45-70 seconds, loop-friendly ending, retro pixel-fantasy mood.

### Stage BGM: undead midgame

Create a seamless loopable dark fantasy strategy game BGM, 80-95 BPM, muted drums, low strings, soft choir texture, eerie but not horror-heavy, supports prolonged tower defense combat, no vocals, no jump scares, 45-70 seconds, clean loop points.

### Stage BGM: late campaign boss

Create a seamless loopable boss-phase fantasy defense track, 95-110 BPM, taiko-style percussion, brass stabs, tense strings, heroic danger, designed for top-down tower defense combat, no vocals, no excessive sub bass, 45-70 seconds, loop-safe transition.

### Arrow Launch

Create a short game-ready arrow shot sound effect, crisp bow release, light wood tension, subtle air pass, no reverb tail, under 0.4 seconds, suitable for rapid repeated playback in a mobile tower defense game.

### Magic Cast

Create a short magical cast wind-up SFX for a support or mage tower, soft arcane shimmer, restrained energy rise, no long tail, under 0.5 seconds, clean layerable sound for frequent use.

### Common Enemy Death

Create three short enemy death sound effects for humanoid fantasy enemies, light armor and cloth elements, restrained pain grunt optional but subtle, no gore, under 0.6 seconds each, suitable for repeated overlapping playback in a pixel fantasy defense game.

### Base Damage Alert

Create a short warning impact sound for the player base taking damage, urgent but not harsh, mix of low hit and warning chime, under 0.7 seconds, should remain readable when other combat sounds are active.
